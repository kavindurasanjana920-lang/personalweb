## About Fardar Express Domestic (FDE)

**Fardar Express Domestic** launched in Sri Lanka in **April 2019**, in collaboration with Fardar International — a Chinese courier company.

Its headline strength is **reach**:

<FeatureGrid items={[
  { icon: "place", title: "All 25 districts", text: "Islandwide network covering every district." },
  { icon: "couriers", title: "90+ branches", text: "One of the wider branch networks locally." },
  { icon: "fast", title: "Real-time updates", text: "Tracking updates through its digital systems." },
]} />

Alongside delivery, FDE runs **cash-on-delivery e-commerce**, **fulfilment and warehousing** for online sellers, **islandwide pickup** services and **final-mile delivery** solutions.

That district coverage is usually the reason a seller picks them. **If your parcel is heading outside the main cities, FDE is often the courier that reaches it.**

## How to Track an FDE / Fardar Express Parcel

The whole process takes **under a minute**. Here is each step.

<StepCard number={1} title="Find your tracking number" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-1-find-tracking-number.webp" alt="Find your FDE tracking number in the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation from the seller">
Check the **SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation** the seller sent you. Sellers label it differently, so look for any of these:

- **Tracking number** — the most common label
- **Waybill number** — used across Sri Lankan couriers
- **Tracking ID** or **AWB number** — same thing, different wording

FDE numbers often start with **`FDE`**, though not always.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Open Trackmate" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-2-open-trackmate.webp" alt="Go to Trackmate.lk and open the parcel tracking interface">
Go to **Trackmate.lk**. The tracking box is right on the homepage.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Select FDE / Fardar Express" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-3-select-courier.webp" alt="Choose FDE or Fardar Express from the list of supported couriers on Trackmate">
Pick **FDE** from the list of supported couriers.

Sellers use both names, so **if you were told "Fardar Express", select FDE** — they are the same company.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={4} title="Enter the tracking details" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-4-enter-tracking-details.webp" alt="Type or paste your FDE tracking or waybill number into Trackmate">
Type or paste your **tracking or waybill number** into the box.

Good news for FDE parcels: **the phone number field is optional**. Unlike Koombiyo, the waybill number alone is usually enough.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={5} title="Review the delivery information" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-5-review-delivery-info.webp" alt="Trackmate tracking results showing FDE parcel status, tracking history timeline and delivery details">
Hit search and review the results — **where your parcel is now, and where it has been**.

You will see the **current status**, a **tracking history timeline** covering pickup through final-mile delivery, and **delivery details** such as the estimated date and destination.
</StepCard>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Tracking to a rural district?">
FDE parcels heading outside the main cities may pass through **more sorting stages** than a Colombo delivery, so extra scans in the timeline are normal rather than a sign of a delay.
</Callout>
