## About Fardar Express Domestic (FDE)

Fardar Express Domestic says it was established in Sri Lanka in April 2019 in collaboration with Fardar International, a Chinese courier company. Its official website highlights an islandwide network covering all 25 districts and currently promotes more than 90 branches, along with parcel tracking and real-time tracking updates through its digital systems. FDE's published services include cash-on-delivery e-commerce, delivery-focused fulfilment and warehousing, islandwide pickup services and final-mile delivery solutions. This gives the FDE section a network, fulfilment and last-mile logistics angle.

## How to Track a FDE / Fardar Express Parcel

**Step 1 — Find your tracking number**

Check the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation sent by the seller. It may be labelled as a tracking number, waybill number, tracking ID or AWB number.

**Step 2 — Open Trackmate**

Go to Trackmate.lk and open the tracking interface.

**Step 3 — Select FDE / Fardar Express**

Choose FDE or Fardar Express from the list of supported couriers on Trackmate.

**Step 4 — Enter the tracking details**

Type or paste the tracking or waybill number. Some couriers may require additional information such as a phone number.

**Step 5 — Check the available tracking information**

Continue to the parcel status and review the delivery information made available for that courier.
