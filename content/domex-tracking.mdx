## About Domex

Domex, operated by Domestic Express, describes itself as a well-established domestic courier service serving destinations across Sri Lanka. Its current website highlights nationwide delivery coverage and a broad mix of courier services. The service portfolio includes e-commerce delivery, special-package handling, bulk delivery, weekend delivery and door-to-door courier services. Domex also promotes cash-on-delivery support for e-commerce customers. This makes the Domex section useful for searchers who may be tracking anything from a normal online order to a special package or business shipment.

## How to Track a Domex Parcel

**Step 1 — Find your tracking number**

Check the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation sent by the seller. It may be labelled as a tracking number, waybill number, tracking ID or AWB number.

**Step 2 — Open Trackmate**

Go to Trackmate.lk and open the tracking interface.

**Step 3 — Select Domex**

Choose Domex from the list of supported couriers on Trackmate.

**Step 4 — Enter the tracking details**

Type or paste the tracking or waybill number. Some couriers may require additional information such as a phone number.

**Step 5 — Check the available tracking information**

Continue to the parcel status and review the delivery information made available for that courier.
