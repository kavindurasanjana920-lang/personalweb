## About Domex

**Domex**, operated by Domestic Express, is one of the **more established** domestic courier services in Sri Lanka, with delivery coverage across the island.

<FeatureGrid items={[
  { icon: "place", title: "Nationwide delivery", text: "Coverage across destinations island-wide." },
  { icon: "couriers", title: "E-commerce & COD", text: "Cash-on-delivery support for online sellers." },
  { icon: "fast", title: "Weekend delivery", text: "Less common among local couriers." },

Beyond the basics, Domex also handles **special-package delivery** for items needing extra care, **bulk delivery** for business shipments, and **door-to-door** courier service.

That range matters when you check a tracking number. A Domex waybill could be **anything from a small Facebook order to a business shipment** — so the status you see depends on the service the sender chose.

## How to Track a Domex Parcel

The whole process takes **under a minute**. Here is each step.

<StepCard number={1} title="Find your tracking number" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-1-find-tracking-number.png" alt="Find your Domex tracking number in the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation from the seller">
Check the **SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation** the seller sent you. Sellers label it differently, so look for any of these:

- **Tracking number** — the most common label
- **Waybill number** — what Domex itself uses
- **Tracking ID** or **AWB number** — same thing, different wording
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Open Trackmate" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-2-open-trackmate.png" alt="Go to Trackmate.lk and open the parcel tracking interface">
Go to **Trackmate.lk**. The tracking box is right on the homepage.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Select Domex" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-3-select-courier.png" alt="Choose Domex from the list of supported couriers on Trackmate">
Pick **Domex** from the list of supported couriers.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={4} title="Enter the tracking details" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-4-enter-tracking-details.png" alt="Type or paste your Domex tracking or waybill number into Trackmate">
Type or paste your **tracking or waybill number** into the box.

Good news for Domex parcels: **the phone number field is optional**. Unlike Koombiyo, the waybill number alone is usually enough.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={5} title="Review the delivery information" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-5-review-delivery-info.png" alt="Trackmate tracking results showing Domex parcel status, tracking history timeline and delivery details">
Hit search and review the results — **where your parcel is now, and where it has been**.

You will see the **current status**, a **tracking history timeline**, and **delivery details** such as the estimated delivery date and destination.
</StepCard>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Keep your waybill number handy">
Save the tracking number somewhere you can find it — a screenshot works. Sellers sometimes delete chat history, and **a tracking number is much harder to recover afterwards**.
</Callout>
