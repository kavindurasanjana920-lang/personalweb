You have the tracking number. What you do not have is **any idea which courier is actually carrying your parcel**.

This happens constantly with online orders. The seller sends a number, you save it for later — and by the time the parcel is due, you cannot remember whether it went through Koombiyo, Domex, TransExpress, Royal Express or FDE.

Here is the honest starting point: **a tracking number on its own does not tell you the courier**. But finding out is usually quick — and it almost always comes down to the message the seller already sent you.

## The Quick Answer

Work through these in order. Most people find the answer at the first one.

1. **Re-read the seller's message.** The courier name is usually right there, a line above or below the tracking number.
2. **Ask the seller.** One message settles it, and it is faster than guessing.

Then open Trackmate, pick that courier, and search.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Do not guess and re-try">
Entering the same number against courier after courier mostly returns "not found" — which tells you nothing, because **a wrong-courier miss and a not-yet-scanned parcel look identical**. Identify the courier first, then search once.
</Callout>

## Check the Seller's Message Again

This solves it more often than anything else, and people skip it because they only scanned for the number the first time.

Go back to the **SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation** and look for:

- **The courier name in plain text** — "sent via Koombiyo", "Domex pickup done"
- **A tracking link** — the domain in the URL names the courier
- **A branch or hub name** — couriers name their hubs in dispatch messages
- **The sender ID on an SMS** — courier SMS often arrives from a named sender, not a number

If the seller runs an online shop, they usually use **the same courier for every order**. So check any older message from that same seller — whatever carried your last parcel probably carries this one.
