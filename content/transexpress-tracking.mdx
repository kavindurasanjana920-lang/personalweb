## About TransExpress

Trans Express Services Lanka presents itself as a Sri Lankan courier provider serving individuals and businesses since 2019. Its company story says the business started in Nugegoda, Colombo, before growing its courier and e-commerce operations. Its service portfolio includes e-commerce and COD delivery, domestic courier services, courier-on-call, next-day delivery, selected same-day delivery, confidential document handling and corporate mailroom support. TransExpress also publishes technology-focused updates, including the use of smarter parcel sorting with IoT-supported processes, giving this section a technology and e-commerce angle that is different from the other courier profiles.

## How to Track a TransExpress Parcel

**Step 1 — Find your tracking number**

Check the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation sent by the seller. It may be labelled as a tracking number, waybill number, tracking ID or AWB number.

**Step 2 — Open Trackmate**

Go to Trackmate.lk and open the tracking interface.

**Step 3 — Select TransExpress**

Choose TransExpress from the list of supported couriers on Trackmate.

**Step 4 — Enter the tracking details**

Type or paste the tracking or waybill number. Some couriers may require additional information such as a phone number.

**Step 5 — Check the available tracking information**

Continue to the parcel status and review the delivery information made available for that courier.
