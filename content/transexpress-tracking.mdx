**Trans Express Services Lanka** has served individuals and businesses since **2019**, starting out in Nugegoda, Colombo before growing its courier and e-commerce operations.

## How to Track a TransExpress Parcel

The whole process takes **under a minute**. Here is each step.

<StepCard number={1} title="Find your tracking number" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-1-find-tracking-number.png" alt="Find your TransExpress tracking number in the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation from the seller">
Check the **SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation** the seller sent you. Sellers label it differently, so look for any of these:

- **Tracking number** — the most common label
- **Waybill number** — used across Sri Lankan couriers
- **Tracking ID** or **AWB number** — same thing, different wording

TransExpress numbers often start with **`TE`**, though not always.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Open Trackmate" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-2-open-trackmate.png" alt="Go to Trackmate.lk and open the parcel tracking interface">
Go to **Trackmate.lk**. The tracking box is right on the homepage.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Select TransExpress" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-3-select-courier.png" alt="Choose TransExpress from the list of supported couriers on Trackmate">
Pick **TransExpress** from the list of supported couriers.

Some sellers write it as **TransEx** or **Trans Express** — all the same courier.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={4} title="Enter the tracking details" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-4-enter-tracking-details.png" alt="Type or paste your TransExpress tracking or waybill number into Trackmate">
Type or paste your **tracking or waybill number** into the box.

Good news for TransExpress parcels: **the phone number field is optional**. Unlike Koombiyo, the waybill number alone is usually enough.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={5} title="Review the delivery information" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-5-review-delivery-info.png" alt="Trackmate tracking results showing TransExpress parcel status, tracking history timeline and delivery details">
Hit search and review the results — **where your parcel is now, and where it has been**.

You will see the **current status**, a **tracking history timeline**, and **delivery details** such as the estimated delivery date and destination.
</StepCard>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Keep your waybill number handy">
Save the tracking number somewhere you can find it — a screenshot works. Sellers sometimes delete chat history, and **a tracking number is much harder to recover afterwards**.
</Callout>
