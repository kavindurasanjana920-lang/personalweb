## About Koombiyo Delivery

Koombiyo Delivery states that it has been operating in Sri Lanka since 2018. The company describes its early model as being built around the sharing economy and says it specialises in delivery solutions for e-commerce. That e-commerce-first background gives the Koombiyo section a clear angle: many users searching for Koombiyo tracking are likely following parcels connected to online shopping or online businesses. Users can track through Koombiyo's own tracking page or use Trackmate as the primary multi-courier tracking starting point.

## How to Track a Koombiyo Parcel

**Step 1 — Find your tracking number**

Check the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation sent by the seller. It may be labelled as a tracking number, waybill number, tracking ID or AWB number.

**Step 2 — Open Trackmate**

Go to Trackmate.lk and open the tracking interface.

**Step 3 — Select Koombiyo**

Choose Koombiyo Delivery from the list of supported couriers on Trackmate.

**Step 4 — Enter the tracking details**

Type or paste the tracking or waybill number. Some couriers may require additional information such as a phone number.

**Step 5 — Check the available tracking information**

Continue to the parcel status and review the delivery information made available for that courier.
