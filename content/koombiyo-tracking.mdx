## About Koombiyo Delivery

**Koombiyo Delivery** has operated in Sri Lanka since **2018**, and its origin story is unusual — the early model was built around the **sharing economy** before the company specialised in e-commerce delivery.

<FeatureGrid items={[

Koombiyo is also **online-business friendly**, which is why so many Facebook, Instagram and TikTok sellers use it.

That e-commerce-first background matters for tracking. **If you are searching for Koombiyo tracking, you are almost certainly following an online order** — a social media purchase, or a delivery from a small online business.

## How to Track a Koombiyo Parcel

The whole process takes **under a minute** once you have both details. Here is each step.

<StepCard number={1} title="Find your tracking number" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-1-find-tracking-number.png" alt="Find your Koombiyo waybill number in the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation from the seller">
Check the **SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation** the seller sent you. Sellers label it differently, so look for any of these:

- **Waybill number** — what Koombiyo itself uses
- **Tracking number** — the most common label
- **Tracking ID** or **AWB number** — same thing, different wording

While you are there, **note the phone number on the order** — you will need it in step 4.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Open Trackmate" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-2-open-trackmate.png" alt="Go to Trackmate.lk and open the parcel tracking interface">
Go to **Trackmate.lk**. The tracking box is right on the homepage.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Select Koombiyo" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-3-select-courier.png" alt="Choose Koombiyo Delivery from the list of supported couriers on Trackmate">
Pick **Koombiyo** from the list of supported couriers.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={4} title="Enter both details" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-4-enter-tracking-details.png" alt="Enter your Koombiyo waybill number and the phone number used on the order into Trackmate">
Type or paste your **waybill number**, then add the **phone number used on the order**.

**Koombiyo requires both.** If the search returns nothing, this is almost always why — check that the phone number matches the one the seller has on file, not necessarily your own.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={5} title="Review the delivery information" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-5-review-delivery-info.png" alt="Trackmate tracking results showing Koombiyo parcel status, tracking history timeline and delivery details">
Hit search and review the results — **where your parcel is now, and where it has been**.

You will see the **current status**, a **tracking history timeline**, and **delivery details** such as the estimated delivery date and destination.
</StepCard>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Whose phone number?">
It is the number **on the order**, which may not be yours. If someone ordered on your behalf, or you gave the seller a different contact number, use that one instead.
</Callout>
