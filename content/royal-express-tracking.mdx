## About Royal Express

**Royal Express Courier & Logistics** is a domestic courier and logistics company built around **fast, affordable delivery**. Founded by people with prior industry experience, it positions itself as a younger and more service-focused operation.

Where Royal Express differs from most local couriers is its **business-side services**: **warehousing**, **bank transportation** and **sensitive-document handling** sit alongside the standard parcel work.

So a Royal Express tracking number could be **a normal online order or a corporate shipment** — the status you see depends on the service the sender chose.

Royal Express also runs its **own online parcel tracking page**, so you have a direct route as well as tracking through Trackmate.

## How to Track a Royal Express Parcel

The whole process takes **under a minute**. Here is each step.

<StepCard number={1} title="Find your tracking number" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-1-find-tracking-number.png" alt="Find your Royal Express tracking number in the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation from the seller">
Check the **SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation** the seller sent you. Sellers label it differently, so look for any of these:

- **Tracking number** — the most common label
- **Waybill number** — used across Sri Lankan couriers
- **Tracking ID** or **AWB number** — same thing, different wording

Royal Express numbers often start with **`REX`**, though not always.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Open Trackmate" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-2-open-trackmate.png" alt="Go to Trackmate.lk and open the parcel tracking interface">
Go to **Trackmate.lk**. The tracking box is right on the homepage.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Select Royal Express" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-3-select-courier.png" alt="Choose Royal Express from the list of supported couriers on Trackmate">
Pick **Royal Express** from the list of supported couriers.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={4} title="Enter the tracking details" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-4-enter-tracking-details.png" alt="Type or paste your Royal Express tracking or waybill number into Trackmate">
Type or paste your **tracking or waybill number** into the box.

Good news for Royal Express parcels: **the phone number field is optional**. Unlike Koombiyo, the waybill number alone is usually enough.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={5} title="Review the delivery information" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-5-review-delivery-info.png" alt="Trackmate tracking results showing Royal Express parcel status, tracking history timeline and delivery details">
Hit search and review the results — **where your parcel is now, and where it has been**.

You will see the **current status**, a **tracking history timeline**, and **delivery details** such as the estimated delivery date and destination.
</StepCard>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Keep your waybill number handy">
Save the tracking number somewhere you can find it — a screenshot works. Sellers sometimes delete chat history, and **a tracking number is much harder to recover afterwards**.
</Callout>
