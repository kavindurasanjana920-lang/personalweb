## About Royal Express

Royal Express Courier & Logistics describes itself as a Sri Lankan domestic courier and logistics company focused on fast and affordable delivery services. The company says it was founded by professionals with industry experience and positions itself as a young, service-focused courier operation. Its published service range includes e-commerce COD, domestic courier, courier-on-call, next-day delivery, warehousing, bank transportation and sensitive-document handling. For tracking, Royal Express provides an online parcel tracking page where customers can enter a package tracking number. This makes the company section useful for both a brief service overview and a direct tracking path.

## How to Track a Royal Express Parcel

**Step 1 — Find your tracking number**

Check the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation sent by the seller. It may be labelled as a tracking number, waybill number, tracking ID or AWB number.

**Step 2 — Open Trackmate**

Go to Trackmate.lk and open the tracking interface.

**Step 3 — Select Royal Express**

Choose Royal Express from the list of supported couriers on Trackmate.

**Step 4 — Enter the tracking details**

Type or paste the tracking or waybill number. Some couriers may require additional information such as a phone number.

**Step 5 — Check the available tracking information**

Continue to the parcel status and review the delivery information made available for that courier.
