## How to Track a Courier Parcel with Trackmate

The whole process takes **under a minute** once you have your tracking number. Here is each step.

<StepCard number={1} title="Find your tracking number" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-1-find-tracking-number.webp" alt="Find your tracking number in the SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation from the seller">
Check the **SMS, WhatsApp message, invoice or order confirmation** the seller sent you. Sellers label it differently, so look for any of these:

- **Tracking number** — the most common label
- **Waybill number** — used by most Sri Lankan couriers
- **Tracking ID** or **AWB number** — same thing, different wording

It usually looks something like `TRK124578LK`.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Open Trackmate" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-2-open-trackmate.webp" alt="Go to Trackmate.lk and open the parcel tracking interface">
Go to **Trackmate.lk**. The tracking box is right on the homepage — no account, no login, no signup.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Select the courier" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-3-select-courier.webp" alt="Choose the courier handling your parcel: Koombiyo, Domex, TransExpress, Royal Express or FDE">
Pick the company handling your parcel — **Koombiyo, Domex, TransExpress, Royal Express or FDE**.

Not sure which one? Check the seller's message again; the courier name is usually mentioned alongside the tracking number.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={4} title="Enter the tracking details" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-4-enter-tracking-details.webp" alt="Type or paste your tracking or waybill number into Trackmate, with a phone number field for couriers that require it">
Type or paste your **tracking or waybill number** into the box.

One thing to watch: **some couriers need a second detail**. Koombiyo, for example, also asks for the phone number used on the order.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={5} title="Review the delivery information" image="/blog/courier-tracking/step-5-review-delivery-info.webp" alt="Trackmate tracking results showing parcel status, tracking history timeline and package details">
Hit search and review the delivery information for that courier — **where your parcel is now, and where it has been**.

You will see the **current status**, a **tracking history timeline**, and **package details** such as the estimated delivery date and destination.
</StepCard>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Save yourself a round trip">
Have **both** your waybill number and the phone number from your order ready before you start. Koombiyo parcels need the phone number, and it is faster than going back to find it mid-search.
</Callout>

## I Have a Tracking Number, but I Don't Know the Courier

**This happens constantly with online orders.** The seller sends a tracking number, you save it for later — and by the time the parcel is due, you have no idea whether it went through Koombiyo, Domex, TransExpress or FDE.

Instead of opening five courier websites one by one, do this:

1. **Open Trackmate** and look at the supported courier list
2. **Check the seller's message again** — the courier name is often right there next to the tracking number
3. **Still unclear?** Message the seller and ask which courier they used, then come back and search

A tracking number on its own will not tell you the courier, so **confirming with the seller is always faster than guessing**.
