### About TransExpress

**Trans Express Services Lanka** has served individuals and businesses since **2019**, starting out in Nugegoda, Colombo before growing its courier and e-commerce operations.

What it offers:

- **E-commerce and COD delivery** — its core business
- **Domestic courier and courier-on-call** — islandwide pickup and drop
- **Next-day delivery**, with same-day available on selected routes
- **Confidential document handling** and corporate mailroom support

TransExpress also leans noticeably technical for a local courier — it publishes updates on **smarter parcel sorting using IoT-supported processes**, which sets it apart from the other couriers on this list.

### About Koombiyo Delivery

**Koombiyo Delivery** has operated in Sri Lanka since **2018**, and its origin story is unusual — the early model was built around the **sharing economy** before the company specialised in e-commerce delivery.

That e-commerce-first background matters for tracking. **If you are searching for Koombiyo tracking, you are almost certainly following an online order** — a Facebook or Instagram purchase, or a delivery from a small online business.

You can track through Koombiyo's own page, or start from Trackmate and search across couriers.

### How to Track a Koombiyo Parcel

Have your **waybill number and phone number** ready — Koombiyo needs both. Use Trackmate as your primary route, with the official Koombiyo page as a backup.

### About Fardar Express Domestic (FDE)

**Fardar Express Domestic** launched in Sri Lanka in **April 2019**, in collaboration with Fardar International — a Chinese courier company.

Its headline strength is **reach**: FDE covers **all 25 districts** through a network of **more than 90 branches**, with real-time tracking updates through its digital systems.

What it offers:

- **Cash-on-delivery e-commerce** delivery
- **Fulfilment and warehousing** for online sellers
- **Islandwide pickup** services
- **Final-mile delivery** solutions

If your parcel is heading somewhere outside the main cities, **FDE's district coverage is the reason a seller likely chose them**.
