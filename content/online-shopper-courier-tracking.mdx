Buy through **Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp** or an online store, and the courier is **the seller's choice — not yours**.

They pick based on the order size, your district, or simply whichever courier they hold an account with. You do not get a say, and you usually do not find out until the tracking message arrives.

The practical result: **your next three purchases could arrive through three different companies.**

## One Bookmark Instead of Five

The usual approach is to search for the courier's website each time a parcel is due — *"Koombiyo tracking"*, *"Domex tracking"*, and so on. It works, but you repeat it for every order, and the search results are not always the official site.

**Keep one starting point instead.** Bookmark Trackmate, open it whenever a parcel is on the way, pick the courier from the list and search.

That is the whole change: **you still need to know your courier, but you stop hunting for a different website every time.**

<Callout variant="tip" title="Where to put the bookmark">
